Flux Clips — Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 July 2026


ABOUT THIS APPLICATION

Flux Clips is a personal, single-user desktop tool. It selects highlight
segments from long-form video, renders them as short vertical clips with
burned-in captions, and publishes the finished files to the operator's own
TikTok account using TikTok's Content Posting API.

There is exactly one operator: the person who built and runs it on their own
computer. The tool does not accept sign-ups, does not act on behalf of other
users, and does not store or share TikTok account data with any third party.


TERMS OF SERVICE

1. Scope

These terms cover the Flux Clips application ("the app") as connected to
TikTok through the TikTok for Developers platform. The app is operated by a
single individual for personal content publishing and is not offered as a
service to the public.

2. What the app is permitted to do

- Read basic profile information for the authorizing account (user.info.basic).
- Upload and publish video files to the authorizing account (video.upload,
  video.publish).

3. What the app will not do

- Post content the operator has not personally selected and reviewed.
- Authenticate or act on behalf of any account other than the operator's own.
- Sell, rent, or share API credentials or tokens with a third party.

4. Responsibility

The operator is solely responsible for the content published through the app
and for compliance with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Developer Terms of
Service.

5. Changes

Because this is a personal tool, these terms may be updated as the tool's
scope changes. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.


PRIVACY POLICY

1. Data collected

The app stores, locally on the operator's own computer only, the OAuth access
token and refresh token issued by TikTok after authorization, plus the minimal
account identifier needed to publish content (open_id). No other personal data
is collected from TikTok or from any viewer of the published videos.

2. Data storage

Tokens are written to a local file on the operator's computer. They are never
transmitted anywhere except directly to TikTok's own API endpoints
(open.tiktokapis.com) to refresh a session or publish a video.

3. Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party, advertiser, or
analytics service. There are no other users of this application besides its
single operator.

4. Data deletion

The operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's account settings, or
delete the local token file to remove all locally stored credentials
immediately.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer account
associated with this application in the TikTok for Developers portal.
